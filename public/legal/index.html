3 · CURBSIDE WAIT BLOCKS, 4-DIGIT BOARDING PIN & PERSONAL PROPERTY

Safety Handshake & Curbside Wait Terms

Every accepted ride generates a unique 4-Digit Bunny Boarding PIN displayed on both the Rider and Pilot portals. Riders must confirm this 4-digit PIN with their assigned pilot at the curb before boarding. Reserved stop wait blocks (15, 30, or 60 minutes) include a 2-minute grace period; wait time beyond the reserved block plus grace period accrues at the disclosed per-minute wait rate. Riders remain solely responsible for personal belongings left inside a vehicle during curbside waits.
